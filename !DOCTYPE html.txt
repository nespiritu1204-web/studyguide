<h1>MATH 11 Study Guide</h1>

<div class="card">
  <h2>Confidence Intervals</h2>

  <h3>CI for a Mean (sigma unknown)</h3>
  <p>
    \[
    \text{CI} = \bar{x} \pm t^\* \left( \frac{s}{\sqrt{n}} \right)
    \]
  </p>

  <h3>CI for a Proportion</h3>
  <p>
    \[
    \hat{p} \pm z^\* \sqrt{ \frac{\hat{p}(1-\hat{p})}{n} }
    \]
  </p>
</div>

<div class="card">
  <h2>1-Sample Hypothesis Tests</h2>

  <h3>Mean Test Statistic</h3>
  <p>
    \[
    t = \frac{\bar{x} - \mu_0}{s/\sqrt{n}}
    \]
  </p>

  <h3>Proportion Test Statistic</h3>
  <p>
    \[
    z = \frac{\hat{p} - p_0}{\sqrt{p_0(1-p_0)/n}}
    \]
  </p>
</div>

<div class="card">
  <h2>Two-Sample Tests</h2>

  <h3>Difference of Two Means</h3>
  <p>
    \[
    (\bar{x}_1 - \bar{x}_2) \pm t^\*
    \sqrt{ \frac{s_1^2}{n_1} + \frac{s_2^2}{n_2} }
    \]
  </p>

  <h3>Difference of Two Proportions</h3>
  <p>
    \[
    \hat{p}_1 - \hat{p}_2 \pm z^\*
    \sqrt{
      \frac{\hat{p}_1(1-\hat{p}_1)}{n_1}
      +
      \frac{\hat{p}_2(1-\hat{p}_2)}{n_2}
    }
    \]
  </p>

  <h3>Pooled Test Statistic</h3>
  <p>
    \[
    p_{\text{pooled}} = \frac{x_1 + x_2}{n_1 + n_2}
    \]
    \[
    z = \frac{(\hat{p}_1 - \hat{p}_2)}
    {\sqrt{p_{\text{pooled}}(1-p_{\text{pooled}})(1/n_1 + 1/n_2)}}
    \]
  </p>
</div>

<div class="card">
  <h2>Matched Pairs</h2>
  <p>
    Differences: \( d = \text{after} - \text{before} \)
  </p>

  <h3>CI</h3>
  <p>
    \[
    \bar{d} \pm t^\*
    \left( \frac{s_d}{\sqrt{n}} \right)
    \]
  </p>

  <h3>Test Statistic</h3>
  <p>
    \[
    t = \frac{\bar{d}}{s_d/\sqrt{n}}
    \]
  </p>
</div>

<div class="card">
  <h2>ANOVA F Statistic</h2>
  <p>
    \[
    F = \frac{MS_{\text{between}}}{MS_{\text{within}}}
    \]
  </p>
</div>

<div class="card">
  <h2>Chi-Square Tests</h2>

  <h3>Goodness of Fit</h3>
  <p>
    \[
    \chi^2 = \sum \frac{(O - E)^2}{E}
    \]
  </p>

  <h3>Expected Counts (Independence)</h3>
  <p>
    \[
    E_{ij} = \frac{(\text{row total})(\text{column total})}{\text{grand total}}
    \]
  </p>
</div>
